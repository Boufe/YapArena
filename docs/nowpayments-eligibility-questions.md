No account approval, provider integration, Canadian legal eligibility, paid plan

**Subject: Written eligibility review — Canadian individual operator, redeemable
internal credits and debate markets**

YapArena is a Canadian personal project, currently operated by an individual.
Before accepting funds, we need a written decision for this exact activity and
flow. Live financial features remain disabled; there is no existing written
Canadian activity/province review or provider approval.

Yap Coins are internal account balances issued by YapArena, not transferable
blockchain tokens: 100 YC = 1 USDC. Users would purchase with crypto, use those
balances in YES/NO debate participation with disclosed randomized pricing and
settlement rules, and may gain or lose value. This is not an ordinary sale of
nonredeemable game credits. Historical play balances stay nonredeemable and
isolated. There are no cards or user-to-user transfers.

The proposed welcome/daily promotional principal stays spend-only. Each grant's
remaining earned profit becomes withdrawal-eligible only after its independent
net-profit target and backing requirements are met. Purchased funds and their
remaining profits have no grant qualification requirement. A separate initial
50-USDC promotional budget is approved but unfunded; customer purchase backing
must not fund promotional rewards.

Withdrawals would be USDC to external wallets, with a 5-USDC gross minimum,
displayed fee inside the gross debit and a positive displayed net amount. There
is no daily account withdrawal cap. YapArena would manually review requests,
targeting two Toronto business days (weekdays excluding Canadian federal
holidays), with chain processing shown separately. Completed
purchases leave through USDC withdrawals; failed, incorrect or reversed deposits
still need support and auditable reconciliation/correction. Error reports are
accepted whenever submitted and reviewed against evidence. Operator-caused
shortfalls and costs use separate operator funds; any attempted recovery with
external costs requires a displayed quote and consent. Ordinary trading losses
remain final. If all stronger authenticators are lost, ordinary email access
must not unlock financial activity without an approved identity/recovery flow.

Please answer these six points explicitly:

1. **Activity, operator and Canada:** Can you approve this actual debate-market,
   redeemable-balance and promotional-profit flow for a Canadian individual
   operator? Do your custody products require a company or other specific legal
   form for this activity? Which Canadian provinces/ages are allowed or excluded,
   and what business/activity evidence is required? Please distinguish your
   policy decision from any legal review YapArena must obtain separately.
2. **Custody and signing:** Which approved product would hold customer backing,
   control deposit/payout keys and sign withdrawals? Who legally owns/controls
   the funds while held, how are customer funds segregated, and what recovery,
   freeze, insolvency, withdrawal-approval and account-termination rules apply?
   We need an exact responsibility split, not a marketing statement that crypto
   payments are supported.
3. **All five input networks and USDC outputs:** Please list actual available
   asset/network IDs and conversion routes for ETH/native USDC on Ethereum,
   ETH/native USDC on Base, SOL/native USDC on Solana, native BNB on BNB Smart
   Chain, and native BTC on Bitcoin. Which native-USDC payout networks/contracts
   are available to this approved account? Do not substitute bridged assets for
   unsupported native USDC. List minimums and any unsupported requested route.
4. **Quotes, finality and corrections:** What quote expiry, spread, exchange and
   network fees apply, when is converted USDC actually settled/available, and
   which confirmation/reorg policy applies to each input? Please explain late,
   under/overpaid, wrong-network, expired and reversed deposits, original-route
   returns where required, cost responsibility and support/dispute deadlines.
   Your public API quote timing and terms describe different timing boundaries;
   which contract controls an actual held quote?
5. **Payout and reconciliation guarantees:** Does an external idempotency ID
   atomically prevent duplicate payout creation after a timeout/concurrent
   retry? How do we query the original operation and obtain transaction/log
   receipts? Which states definitively prove payment or no payment, including
   payouts that report `failed` and later resume? We retain reserves for unknown
   outcomes and must reconcile before replacing a payment or releasing funds.
6. **Identity, retention, price and supported testing:** Who performs user and
   operator identity/sanctions checks, at what thresholds, with what Canadian
   residency and retention/deletion requirements? What minimum plan, fees,
   limits or reserve/hold commitments apply? Is there a maintained free sandbox
   for these exact products/routes? The linked legacy sandbox collection says
   it has not been maintained since 2025; please identify the current supported
   test environment and its limitations. No paid onboarding or live transaction
   is authorized by this inquiry.

Please return a written yes/no decision with route-specific restrictions,
prerequisites, applicable terms/version and the smallest next onboarding step.
If the activity or operator is ineligible, say so directly; we will not disguise
the activity or route around that decision.
