## Behavior

Price, median endpoints/interval, final share and own-limit markers remain mounted
and glide to each inspected round's values over 320 ms with an ease-out curve.
Rapid hovering retargets the transition from its current position. Initial data
appears at its actual location; missing or crossed intervals retain their explicit
unavailable/nonpositive explanation. Polling keeps the inspected round and does
not briefly reset the scale to the latest round.

The hovered round's argument replaces the debate proposition directly beneath
“What fraction of participants endorse.” Outgoing text fades upward and incoming
text slides into place. Reserved heading space prevents different label lengths
from moving the graph under the pointer. Leaving the graph restores the debate
proposition; the numeric YES/NO badges and scale restore the latest settled round.
Reduced-motion preferences disable both animations. The argument bubble and
diamonds below the X axis are removed; ordinary round tick labels remain.

The same components serve live and replay. Revealed history, privacy boundaries,
commitment targets, pricing and countdown behavior are unchanged. This revision
requires no database migration, dependency or hosting configuration change.
