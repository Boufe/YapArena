# Homepage video hero — October 16, 2026

Superseding note — 2026-10-19: PRs #31–34 are merged. The temporary logo-only `/`
page described in this trial was replaced by [the wide homepage](../../wide-homepage.md);
the hero/grid remain at `/live`. Deployment details below describe their dated handoff.

At the [Live-page checkpoint](live-feed-2026-10-16.md), this hero and discovery
content moved to `/live`, while `/` temporarily became the logo page. This report
preserves the original hero trial; the later wide homepage supersedes that root layout.

The [featured preview contract](../../homepage-preview.md) replaces the homepage
illustration with a real, muted live/replay preview in the existing navy theme.
The debate question, topic and public speakers replace film-style branding. Hover
emphasizes the video and offers a cancellable 2.6-second entry countdown; click,
tap and keyboard Enter open the ordinary watching interface. There is no Watch
now button. Reduced motion disables automatic entry and playback, while explicit
Play preview remains available.

Selection ranks recent playable debates by settled human Yap Coins premium
volume, giving live debates priority and breaking ties by recency. It does not use
likes, reservations, pending/unrevealed results or private bot/observer activity.
An eligible replay is labeled when nothing live is eligible. No media/provider
calls block HTML rendering; SDK loading and playback begin after page load/idle
time. The preview releases media offscreen, in background/offline and on exit,
has a pause control, and bounds playback/startup time and HLS buffering.
