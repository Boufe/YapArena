## Limits

Synthetic argument rates are independently simulated, not human votes. Seeded
history is a presentation feed, not wallet/submission/journal activity. Later
mixed/observer aggregates come from finally revealed A4 settlements only.
The public window is 100 points; refresh is every five seconds while visible and
homepage updates wait for active inspection to finish. See the
[data contract and operator procedure](../../shared-bot-discovery.md).

No hosted browser account journey, physical-device, load, provider Data API,
GraphQL or Realtime isolation trial is claimed here. Hosted functional acceptance
remains the user's manual test. CI and deployment evidence belong to the PR
handoff; additive tables/history remain on source rollback.
