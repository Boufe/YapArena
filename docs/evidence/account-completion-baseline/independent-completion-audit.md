Current official
[Privy support policy](https://docs.privy.io/user-management/users/managing-users/supporting-your-users)
states that losing a sole login method cannot restore the original provider
account. YapArena's approved recovery instead verifies a distinct sole-email
Privy identity and requires its own retained security key to replace the local
mapping. The old DID becomes a permanent tombstone. Neither a matching email,
new bearer-token issuance time nor weaker email recovery authorizes the change.
An already-bound new DID fails; no provider account is merged, deleted or
unilaterally changed. If all retained strong application methods are lost, ordinary email access may
remain available but operations requiring their proof are inaccessible. Email
cannot reset or replace those credentials. The application cannot detect physical
loss and does not automatically impose an account-wide hold; a provider-approved
manual recovery procedure remains unimplemented. Genuine Privy delivery,
OAuth and the complete hosted recovery ceremony remain untested here.
